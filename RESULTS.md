# Results

## Paper summary

1. **Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology Commission (2025)**: 58 international experts used a Delphi consensus process to define clinical and preclinical obesity, set diagnostic criteria based on more than BMI, and describe remission as a better treatment goal than weight loss alone. [docs/papers/PIIS2213858724003164/PIIS2213858724003164.md]

2. **Abdullah et al., BMJ Open 2014**: In 5,036 Framingham Heart Study participants, an 'obese-years' measure (how obese you are multiplied by how long) was linked to cardiovascular disease risk and fit the data better than obesity level or duration alone. [docs/papers/e005629.full/e005629.full.md]

3. **Reis et al., Obesity 2015 (CARDIA)**: In 4,061 US young adults followed for 25 years, years of excess BMI and years of excess waist size were both associated with new cardiovascular disease. [docs/papers/Obesity - 2015 - Reis - Excess body mass index‐ and waist circumference‐years and incident cardiovascular disease  The/...md]

4. **ECOS multi-cohort study (PIIS2589537026004840)**: Using data from about 787,000 people in high-income countries, it estimated how common clinical and preclinical obesity are. Clinical obesity was less common than BMI-defined obesity in children and more common from mid-adulthood on, with differences between sexes. [docs/papers/PIIS2589537026004840/PIIS2589537026004840.md]
